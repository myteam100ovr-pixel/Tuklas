# Tuklas Terms of Use

Updated 5 November 2026 · Project draft pending final operator and public contact details.

## Scope and operator

These terms describe responsible use of Tuklas by visitors, youth, trainers, and administrators, including mobile users. Tuklas is a Tour de Force project at Universidad de Dagupan supporting career guidance and skills development in Bugallon, Pangasinan. The project team is responsible for confirming its legal operator identity and public contact before production use. These pages do not establish official endorsement by TESDA, PESO, or the University.

## Accounts and age

Provide accurate details and keep your password, authentication token, and recovery codes private. Use only accounts and roles assigned to you. Youth services are intended for ages 15–30; users under 18 should review this notice and the privacy notice with a parent or guardian. The current system does not independently verify age or guardian consent. Administrators must obtain any required permissions before enrolling minors or processing their information.

## Respect other users' data

Upload information you own or are authorized to submit, and remove unnecessary personal information about other people. Do not impersonate users, scrape personal records, bypass role checks, share account credentials, submit malicious files, harass others, or use the service unlawfully.

Trainers and administrators must access information only for assigned career, training, and account-management duties. Do not publish, sell, or share personal records outside authorized work. Report access errors, exposed records, or suspected misuse to the project team. Users retain their rights in their submitted content; Tuklas may process it only for disclosed service purposes and applicable legal obligations.

## Guidance and external services

AI scans, assessments, career suggestions, and chat replies may be incomplete or incorrect. They are guidance and do not establish qualifications, hiring decisions, admission, scholarships, or training availability. Verify important information with the relevant employer, school, or training provider. Request human review of inaccurate recommendations rather than relying on an automated result alone.

Using AI features sends information to the configured external AI service as described in the [privacy notice](/privacy-policy). External websites and sign-in providers operate under their own terms. Do not provide sensitive information that is unnecessary for career guidance.

## Account restrictions and closure

Authorized administrators may deactivate or delete accounts to manage the service or respond to misuse. Contact the project team to ask for review of a restriction or correction of an error. Account deletion currently removes access through a reversible soft deletion; it does not automatically erase every stored record. See the privacy notice for retained records and privacy requests.

## Privacy and user rights

Read the [privacy notice](/privacy-policy) for data categories, staff access, AI sharing, cookies, and retention limitations. These terms do not waive statutory privacy rights or authorize unrelated data use. Publishing these terms is not proof that a user accepted them: the current application does not keep a versioned acceptance record covering all registration methods. Separate informed consent must be obtained where required.

## Availability and responsibility

Features depend on hosting, connectivity, and configured service providers and may be interrupted or changed. Keep your own copies of important documents. Nothing in these terms excludes rights or responsibilities that cannot lawfully be excluded, or removes the operator's duty to protect personal data.

## Questions and revisions

Contact the Tour de Force project team through the person who provided your access. A dedicated public contact remains to be supplied before production use. The team should notify users of material changes and seek renewed agreement or consent where required. Philippine law and applicable data-protection rights remain relevant; users may raise privacy complaints with the [National Privacy Commission](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-subject-rights/).
